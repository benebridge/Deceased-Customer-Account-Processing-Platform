Understanding Grief

Grief is a natural response to loss. There's no "right" way to grieve, and everyone's journey is unique. Some common experiences include:

  • Denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and acceptance (not necessarily in order)
  • Physical symptoms like fatigue, changes in appetite, or difficulty sleeping
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • Waves of intense emotion that come and go
  • Feeling numb or disconnected

Remember: Seeking support is a sign of strength, not weakness. Professional counseling can provide tools and strategies to help you navigate this difficult time.
